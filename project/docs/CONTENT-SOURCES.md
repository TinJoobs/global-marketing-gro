## Deliberate differences from the mockup

- Two core solutions, not four equally positioned core offers, because the document explicitly centers Sales Growth System and Virtual Assistant Services.
- The document's five-part Sales Growth process and five-step VA matching process, rather than treating the mockup's six-stage process as approved service copy.
- No 20,000+ leads, 15,000+ automations, 75,000+ hours, 150+ staff, 1,000+ systems, 98% satisfaction, 50+ industries, or 5+ years claims. These were not verified by the primary content document.
- No unverified star ratings or review-count claims.
- No old GMGSB branding, conflicting limited-time discounts, or unsupported free-trial offer.
- No implied guaranteed results, constant VA availability, security certification, or regulated-profession service promise.
- The $10/hour rate is explicitly a starting rate for general VA support, not a price for the whole combined service.
- The additional AI page discusses scoped, human-supervised possibilities, not a guaranteed feature bundle or Humach product capability.
