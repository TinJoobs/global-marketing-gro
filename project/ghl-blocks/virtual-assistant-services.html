Common questions

Build confidence before you delegate.

Can a Virtual Assistant manage the CRM?
Yes. Depending on your service agreement, a Virtual Assistant can update records, monitor inquiries, manage follow-up tasks, move leads through pipeline stages, confirm appointments, and prepare reports.
How much do Virtual Assistant services cost?
General Virtual Assistant support starts at $10 per hour. Specialized or certified support may require a different rate depending on the qualifications and scope of work required.
How do you select a Virtual Assistant?
We conduct a discovery and needs-assessment process, identify the required skills and schedule, and match the client with a Virtual Assistant suited to the role.
Where are your Virtual Assistants located?
Our Virtual Assistants are based in the Philippines and support clients remotely.
Can I use Virtual Assistant services without purchasing the Sales Growth System?
Yes. Virtual Assistant support can be used as a separate service or integrated with the Sales Growth System.
