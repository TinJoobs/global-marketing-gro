## Presentation requirements

- Use `object-fit: contain`; retain the original colors and proportions, with breathing room around every asset.
- QuickBooks uses its official dark-ink wordmark; place its image on the continuous light neutral strip.
- Keep the strip free of visible service labels; provide accessible link names. Duplicate items used for animation should be hidden from assistive technology and keyboard focus.
- Honor reduced-motion preferences and allow the moving strip to pause for interaction.
- Shopify’s mark and written name must link to https://www.shopify.com/. See the official [brand assets](https://www.shopify.com/brand-assets) and [trademark guidance](https://www.shopify.com/legal/trademarks).
- Meta kit downloads were not requested through terms checkboxes; the documented first-party website icons were used. No login, account creation, terms checkbox, or partner badge was used.
